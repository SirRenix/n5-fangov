    duty is the PWM value 0–255 the daemon writes (100 % = 255). Each cycle a channel moves from its current duty towards the curve's target in step_up/step_down steps. critical forces 100 % at once; a stall kicks the channel up until it spins. stop is the duty left when the daemon stops. Curves are stored in °C.

      n5-fangov  beta
      
          
      A fan governor for small servers whose EC or Super-I/O exports its fan outputs through a kernel hwmon driver. The daemon takes over the pwm channels the driver exposes, drives each from its own curve with a selectable temperature source, slews between steps and stands guard: a critical temperature forces full duty, a stall kicks the channel up, a systemd watchdog restarts a hung daemon and the failsafe leaves the channels at a safe duty when it stops. This dashboard and the n5-fangov CLI share one API.
